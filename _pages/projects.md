### National Research and Policy Advisory / 国家科研与政策咨询

1. **信息-物理-社会视角下数据驱动的区域能源系统优化运行**  
   *Data-Driven Optimization and Operation of Regional Energy Systems from an Information-Physical-Social Perspective*  
   国家自然科学基金国际合作与交流项目 / International Cooperation and Exchange Program, National Natural Science Foundation of China | 2022-2025

2. **全国统一电力市场规范化建设与评价体系研究**  
   *Research on the Standardized Development and Evaluation Framework of the National Unified Electricity Market*  
   国家发展和改革委员会体制改革综合司 / Department of Economic System Reform, National Development and Reform Commission | 2025

3. **深化全国统一电力市场体系建设关键环节机制研究——电网调度运行机制研究**  
   *Research on Key Mechanisms for Deepening the National Unified Electricity Market System: Power Grid Dispatch and Operation Mechanisms*  
   国家发展和改革委员会体制改革综合司 / Department of Economic System Reform, National Development and Reform Commission | 2024

### Electricity Market and Power System Transition / 电力市场与新型电力系统转型

   *Transition Cost Analysis, Market Bidding Mechanisms, and Validation for New Power Systems under a Multi-Value Framework*  
   国家电网公司总部科技攻关项目 / State Grid Corporation of China Headquarters Research Project | 2023-2025

   国家电网公司总部科技项目 / State Grid Corporation of China Headquarters Research Project | 2022-2024

6. **面向新型电力系统的爬坡辅助服务市场关键技术研究及应用**

   国家电网公司科技项目 / State Grid Corporation of China Research Project | 2023-2024

7. **新型电力系统灵活爬坡能力评估方法及市场机制研究**  
   *Assessment Methods and Market Mechanisms for Flexible Ramping Capability in New Power Systems*  
   国家电网公司科技项目 / State Grid Corporation of China Research Project | 2023-2024
